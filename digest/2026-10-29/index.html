Lowe's kicks off Black Friday, Home Depot sets Nov 5 – 25, DreamHost drops to $1.99/mo

Oct 29, 2026 · Prices verified on official pages; last checked Oct 29, 2026

Tools: Lowe’s and The Home Depot

  • Lowe’s started a four-week Black Friday sale today (Oct 29) covering tools, appliances and outdoor power equipment — some brands add a free tool with select battery kits. “12 Days of Deals” starts Nov 16 and Black Friday Doorbusters open to everyone on Nov 27 (MyLowe’s Rewards members get online access a day early). Lowe’s didn’t list item prices.
  • The Home Depot announced its Black Friday Savings Event for Nov 5 – Nov 25, in stores and online. The headline for us: buy one, get a select tool free on RYOBI, Milwaukee and DEWALT power tools. Appliances get up to $500 extra off (max $1,250 total).

Hosting

  • DreamHost has 4-year shared hosting from $1.99/mo (Launch, save 83%), with Growth at $3.49/mo and Scale at $7.99/mo. It’s billed upfront for the full term and renews at $7.99 / $8.99 / $19.99 per month. The page calls it an October special — it isn’t labeled Black Friday, so a lower Black Friday price is possible but not confirmed.
  • Hostinger prices are unchanged from yesterday. We added the rest of its Black Friday lineup: web apps hosting $3.49/mo, AI automation apps $4.99/mo, Hostinger Agent $6.99/mo, email marketing $0.99/mo and ecommerce $4.99/mo.

No change

NordVPN and Surfshark prices are the same as yesterday. Amazon still hasn’t published its Black Friday dates.
