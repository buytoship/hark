About Stack Daily

Stack Daily is a daily deals digest for US shoppers who buy tech: VPNs and security software, web hosting and VPS, AI tools, electronics, and the apps that pay you back.

How we pick deals

  • Official pages only. A price goes on the site only after we see it on the merchant’s or retailer’s own website or press release.
  • No guessing. If a merchant doesn’t publish an original price, a start date or an end date, we write “unknown” instead of filling it in.
  • The fine print up front. Hosting and VPN deals usually need a long prepaid term and renew at a higher price. We show the term and the renewal price whenever the merchant publishes it.
  • Dated checks. Every deal shows the date we last verified it. Prices change — always confirm at checkout.
